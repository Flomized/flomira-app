Trust, Transparency, and Data Care

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 21, 2026

1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the FLOMIRA web platform (https://flomira.onrender.com) and the FLOMIRA mobile bakery management application (com.example.demoflomira). By accessing or using FLOMIRA, you acknowledge and agree to the practices outlined in this policy.

2. Google API Services & Google Drive User Data

The FLOMIRA mobile application integrates with Google Drive via Google Sign-In to provide backup and restore capabilities for bakery store administrators. We strictly follow Google's API Services User Data Policy.

A. Requested Scope

FLOMIRA requests only the following specific Google OAuth scope:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file

B. How Google Drive Data is Used

  • File-Specific Isolation: The drive.file scope only permits access to files created directly by the FLOMIRA application. FLOMIRA cannot view, edit, access, or delete any other files or folders in your Google Drive.
  • Dedicated Backup Storage: FLOMIRA creates a dedicated folder named "Flomira Backups" in your Google Drive to store database snapshots (orders, recipes, ingredients, and costs).
  • Restore Functionality: When requested by the administrator, FLOMIRA downloads these backup files from your dedicated folder to restore bakery data.

C. Data Sharing & Transfer Restrictions

  • No Third-Party Sharing: Google user data obtained via Google APIs is never shared, transferred, sold, or disclosed to third parties, external advertisers, or brokers.
  • No AI/ML Model Training: Google user data is not used to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • Direct Encrypted Connection: All communication occurs directly between your mobile device and Google's servers over secure HTTPS/TLS.

D. Google Limited Use Compliance Statement

FLOMIRA's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

E. User Control & Revocation

You can disconnect Google Drive at any time inside the app settings, or revoke access completely via your Google Account Security Permissions. You may also delete backup files directly from your Google Drive at any time.

3. Web Storefront Data Handling

On our web storefront (https://flomira.onrender.com):

  • Cart & Drafts: Cart items and order planning drafts are stored locally on your device browser profile. They are not transmitted to tracking networks.
  • Inquiry Details: When you submit an order request or custom inquiry, provided contact information (name, phone, delivery preferences) is used exclusively to contact you regarding your order.
  • No Financial Data Stored: We do not process or retain credit card or payment credentials on this website.

4. Data Security

We implement standard technical and organizational security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. All network communication uses HTTPS encryption.
